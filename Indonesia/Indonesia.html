Indonesia offers exhilarating hiking experiences with active volcanoes like Mount Rinjani, Mount Bromo, and Mount Semeru, providing breathtaking views and adventurous treks.

Explore Indonesia's underwater wonders in Raja Ampat, Bali’s Tulamben, and other marine hotspots, known for their rich biodiversity and vibrant coral reefs.

Feel the adrenaline rush with skydiving over Bali and Batam Island, white-water rafting on the Ayung River and Citarik River, and world-class surfing in Uluwatu, Kuta, and the Mentawai Islands.

Best Spots for Extreme Sports Lover

Mount Rinjani, Lombok

Mount Rinjani, on Lombok Island, stands at 3,726 meters. This active volcano offers stunning views and challenging treks through lush forests and the Segara Anak crater lake. It's a must-visit for adventure seekers.

Raja Ampat Islands, Papua

Raja Ampat, in West Papua, is a diver's paradise. Known for the highest marine biodiversity on Earth, it offers vibrant coral reefs, clear waters, and encounters with manta rays and whale sharks. Perfect for underwater explorers.

Pangandaran, West Java

Pangandaran, located in West Java, is a popular destination for skydiving enthusiasts. Known for its stunning black sand beaches and beautiful coastal views, it offers tandem jumps from 10,000 feet, providing an exhilarating experience and breathtaking aerial scenery2. It's a must-try for thrill-seekers looking to add an unforgettable adventure to their trip
