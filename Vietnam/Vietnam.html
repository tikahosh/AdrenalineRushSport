Explore the stunning landscapes of Vietnam by hiking through its diverse terrain. Discover picturesque mountains, lush forests, and serene rice terraces. Enjoy trails that cater to all skill levels, offering breathtaking views and encounters with unique wildlife. Experience the rich cultural heritage found in traditional villages along the way. From the terraced fields of Sapa to the scenic trails of Ba Be National Park, each hike promises a memorable adventure. Feel the connection to nature and the thrill of discovery with every step you take.

Dive into the clear, turquoise waters of Vietnam, home to vibrant coral reefs and diverse marine life. Explore top diving spots like Nha Trang, Con Dao, and Phu Quoc. Swim alongside colorful fish, explore intricate coral gardens, and uncover fascinating underwater caves. Ideal for both beginners and experienced divers, Vietnam's marine world offers thrilling and awe-inspiring adventures. Discover the underwater beauty that makes every dive unforgettable and enjoy the rich marine ecosystem with each dive.

Experience the thrill of skydiving over Vietnam's breathtaking landscapes. Jump from high above and feel the adrenaline rush as you free-fall before gliding down with a parachute. Enjoy stunning views of Vietnam's coastline, lush greenery, and picturesque islands below. Tandem jumps with professional instructors ensure a safe and exhilarating experience. Feel the excitement of soaring through the sky and creating memories that will last a lifetime.

Best Spots for Extreme Sports Lover

Hiking in Mount Fansipan, Sapa

Fansipan Mountain, also known as the "Roof of Indochina," is the highest peak in Vietnam, standing at 3,147 meters. Located near Sapa Town in Lao Cai Province, it offers breathtaking views and a challenging trek. You can either hike the mountain, which takes about 2 days, or take the Sun World Fansipan Legend cable car for a quicker ascent. The cable car ride offers stunning views of the surrounding rice terraces and mountains. At the summit, you'll find serene gardens, pagodas, and a celestial Buddha statue. The panoramic views from the top are truly unforgettable.

Diving in Cham Island, Quảng Nam

Cham Island, also known as Cu Lao Cham, is a UNESCO marine park located off the coast of Hoi An. It's a paradise for divers and snorkelers, with 135 species of corals and over 200 tropical fish species. The island offers a variety of dive sites, from coral gardens to deep water pinnacles. Cham Island Diving Center provides PADI courses and guided dives, making it suitable for both beginners and experienced divers. The crystal-clear waters and vibrant marine life create an unforgettable diving experience.

Try Skydive in Hanoi

Skydiving in Hanoi is an exhilarating experience offered by Skydive Vietnam. Located about 35 kilometers southwest of the city center, Skydiving Hill 833 is the perfect spot for tandem jumps. Jumping from an altitude of 4,500 meters, you'll experience 60 seconds of freefall and a 5-minute canopy ride. The instructors are internationally qualified, ensuring a safe and thrilling adventure7. You can also capture the moment with video and photo packages. The stunning views of the Vietnamese landscape from above make this an unforgettable experience
